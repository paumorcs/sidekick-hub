You are a content production engine for a YouTube channel called "Need to Know Man" — a thought-provoking documentary-style channel that explores theology, secret societies, geopolitics, conspiracy, philosophy, and hidden history. Theology & Religion — Jesus: Man, God, or Both? / Trinitarian vs Non-Trinitarian / Religion vs Faith 2. Secret Societies & Esoteric — Freemasonry (Regular vs Liberal/GoDF), hidden history, symbols 3. Geopolitics & Power — Capitalism vs Communism, ICC, global power structures 6. Science & Society — Climate skepticism, AI vs Creativity, fitness culture VIDEO FORMAT: - Standard: 20 minutes = 4,000–4,500 words - Long-form: 30–45 minutes = 6,000–9,000 words - Hook in first 30 seconds: provocative question or shocking claim - Structure: Hook → Context → Deep Dive (3 perspectives) → The Hidden Layer → Conclusion that opens debate - No conclusions forced — end with the question, not the answer SCRIPT STYLE: - Write like a documentary narrator: authoritative but not arrogant - Use rhetorical questions to drive tension - Vary sentence length for rhythm — short punches after long builds - No academic jargon — but never dumb it down - Every segment should end with a micro-cliffhanger or pivot VISUAL DIRECTION: - 3D characters: dark-skinned mannequin-style figures OR realistic 3D human models in dramatic poses - Environments: shadowed rooms, ancient stone, candlelit settings, digital data environments - Color palette: deep blacks, dark blues, gold accents, occasional red for emphasis - Mood: cinematic, serious, slightly ominous — like a premium Netflix documentary When I say NEXT, move to the next stage of production. Step-by-Step: Creating a 20-Min AI Video
Follow these steps in order for each video you produce.
  1. 1
    Initialize Claude Code
    Download Claude Desktop, open the sidebar (three lines top-left), switch to Claude Code mode. Paste your Master Prompt from the first tab and send it. Claude will confirm it understands your channel identity.
    Claude Desktop Claude Code
  2. 2
    Choose Your Topic & Collect Reference Transcripts
    Pick one topic from your list. Find 2–3 YouTube videos in a similar niche (dark documentary, conspiracy, theology), open their transcripts (click "Show transcript" under the video), copy them, and save into a PDF. Upload the PDF to Claude Code.
    YouTube Transcripts Google Docs / PDF
    The more reference transcripts you give Claude, the better it learns your niche's rhythm, pacing, and tone.
  3. 3
    Generate Your Script
    Tell Claude your topic and ask for a script. Include a word count in brackets. For a 20-minute video, request 4,000–4,500 words. Claude will write a full documentary-style script following your channel's tone and structure.
    Claude Code
    Formula: ~200 words = 1 minute of video. 4,000 words = 20 minutes.
  4. 4
    Clone Your Voice & Generate Voiceover
    Upload 1–3 minutes of your own voice to ElevenLabs to create a voice clone. Paste your script into ElevenLabs and generate the full narration audio. Download the MP3.
    ElevenLabs (~$5/mo)
    Your real cloned voice builds more audience trust on thought-provoking content than a generic AI voice.
  5. 5
    Upload Sample Visuals to Claude
    Take 3–5 screenshots from dark documentary YouTube channels with the 3D/cinematic style you want. Upload them to Claude Code and type "images attached." Claude will analyze the visual style and use it to generate prompts.
    Claude Code
  6. 6
    Generate Image Prompts for Every Scene
    Ask Claude: "Generate image prompts for every scene in my script." For a 20-minute video you'll get 300–400+ scene prompts, each matched to the corresponding script section. Save all prompts to a Google Doc.
    Claude Code
  7. 7
    Generate Scene Images
    Copy each image prompt into Google Flow (free tier available, ~$2/mo for full access). Use Flux or Imagen model. Upload a reference image for the first scene — this ensures your 3D character stays consistent across all scenes by using it as a style reference for subsequent prompts.
    Google Flow (~$2/mo) Whisk (free) Higgsfield / Open Art
    Always upload a reference image for character consistency. Without it your 3D character will look different in every scene.
  8. 8
    Generate Animation Prompts & Animate Scenes
    Ask Claude: "Generate video animation prompts for each scene." Then in Google Flow, switch to the video/VOT model, upload each scene image, paste the animation prompt, and generate short video clips (3–5 seconds each). Download all clips.
    Google Flow VOT model Claude Code
    VOT video generation costs ~20 credits per clip on Google Flow. Budget accordingly for a 20-min video with many scenes.
  9. 9
    Create Your Thumbnail
    Upload 3–5 thumbnail screenshots from top channels in your niche to Claude Code. Type "thumbnails attached." Claude will generate thumbnail concepts and prompts. Take your chosen prompt to Google Flow, attach a reference thumbnail, and generate yours.
    Claude Code Google Flow Canva (text overlay)
  10. 10
    Edit & Assemble the Final Video
    Import all your animated scene clips and the voiceover MP3 into CapCut or DaVinci Resolve. Sync the visuals to the narration, add background music (dark ambient/cinematic), color grade to your dark palette, and export at 1080p or 4K.
    DaVinci Resolve (free) CapCut (free) Pixabay / Freesound (music)
    For dark documentary style: reduce highlights, crush blacks slightly, add a subtle film grain overlay in post.
  11. 11
    Write Title, Description & Tags with Claude
    Back in regular Claude (not Code), paste your script and ask for: 5 title options, a SEO-optimized YouTube description, 20 relevant tags, and 3 pinned comment ideas to seed the debate in your comment section.
    Claude (this chat)
  12. 12
    Upload & Publish
    Upload your video to YouTube. Use your generated description and tags. Set your thumbnail. Schedule or publish. Post your pinned comment immediately after going live to spark the debate before anyone else comments.
    YouTube Studio

Time Estimate Per Video
Script generation: 15–30 min · Voiceover: 30–60 min · Image generation (300+ scenes): 3–6 hrs · Animation: 2–4 hrs · Editing: 3–5 hrs · Total: 10–16 hrs per 20-min video starting out. This drops significantly as you build templates and batching routines.
Monthly Cost Estimate (1 User)
Claude Pro: $20 · ElevenLabs: $5 · Google Flow: $2–5 · Total: approximately $27–30/month for the full stack. DaVinci Resolve and CapCut are free.
Pro Tip: Batch Your Production
Don't produce one video at a time. Write 3–4 scripts in one Claude session, generate all image prompts together, then batch your image generation across a few days. This cuts per-video time dramatically once you're past the learning curve.
